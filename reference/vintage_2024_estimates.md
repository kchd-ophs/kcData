# Vintage 2024 population estimates

Kansas City population estimates for 2021-2024. According to the [US
Census
Bureau](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/popest/2020s-total-cities-and-towns.html),
"Vintage 2024 is the most recent completed vintage and consistent set of
estimates."

## Usage

``` r
vintage_2024_estimates
```

## Format

A dataframe with 5 rows and 2 columns.

- year:

  Year

- estimate:

  Resident population estimate as of July 1 of the year

## Source

[US Census Bureau, City and Town Intercensal Population Totals:
2020-2024](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/popest/2020s-total-cities-and-towns.html)
